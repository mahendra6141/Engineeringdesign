Hoist Motor Calculation
This section explains the engineering calculations used for Main Hoist (MH) and Auxiliary Hoist (AH) motor power determination.
Mechanism Hoist
Mechanisms MH + AH
Calculation Type Motor Power
Calculation Basis Engineering Calculation
Why this calculation is required?

The hoist motor calculation determines the power required to lift the specified load at the rated hoisting speed while considering mechanical efficiency, service factor, electrical power margin and derating factor.

03. Mechanical Power
Mechanical Power Pmech
Purpose

Mechanical power required for lifting the calculated total weight at the rated hoisting speed is determined.

Formula
Pmech = M × V / (6.12 × E)

04. Required Motor Power
Required Electrical Motor Power Pele
Purpose

Required motor power is obtained by applying the service factor, electrical power margin and ambient derating factor to the calculated mechanical power.

Formula
Pele = Pmech × S × (1 + Margin / 100) / Camb
